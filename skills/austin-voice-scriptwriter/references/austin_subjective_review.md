# Austin-owned full-text review

Review the complete body as a listener, not as a checklist.

- What is the first thing the speaker is actually looking at?
- Where does the argument change because of the current material?
- Which sentences sound like a consultant, a product brief, or a generic AI blogger?
- Does the rhythm have a human point of view without borrowing a fixed catchphrase?
- Does the final stop answer this topic's pressure, or could it be pasted onto another topic?

This review is one outer-Codex reread with at most one rewrite for the current item. Counts and string scans are diagnostic only.
