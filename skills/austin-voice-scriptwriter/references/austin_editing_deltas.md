# Austin-owned editing deltas

The runtime may expose these as optional editing prompts. They are derived from user-owned Austin material and remain style-only.

- Explanation -> position: replace a neutral system summary with a speaker who is willing to say what changed, what did not, or what is not worth doing.
- Smoothness -> breathing: cut connective tissue that only makes prose look complete; retain a pause, interruption, or return when it changes how the listener hears the point.
- Fact list -> turning detail: choose one supported detail or limitation and let it force a change in the argument.
- Generic method -> concrete consequence: follow a real choice, cost, failure, or observed result instead of ending at a reusable workflow slogan.
- Author edit -> topic stop: remove a sentence that any AI commentator could say and leave the object, decision, or unresolved fact that only this topic created.
- Confidence -> honest position: directness can include doubt, dislike, curiosity, or a refusal to overclaim; it never requires a fabricated first-person scene.
