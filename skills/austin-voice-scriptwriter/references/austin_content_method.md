# Austin-owned content method

This is a safe, Git-owned summary of user-owned writing practice. It does not contain private case text or a complete exemplar.

- Begin with the material that creates pressure, not with a tool definition or a polished thesis supplied by an editorial card.
- Let the source's concrete object, action, limitation, person, or consequence decide the movement of the piece.
- Keep the author's position visible when the material supports it. When it does not, use direct argument, public attribution, observation, or recommendation instead of invented experience.
- Let background arrive while the speaker is thinking about the current object. Do not turn the spoken body into a report, workflow manual, or universal AI conclusion.
- Stop at the question, choice, consequence, or discovery that belongs to this item. A complete ending is not a required slogan.
